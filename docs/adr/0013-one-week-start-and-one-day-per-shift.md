# One week starts where the workplace says, and every shift renders on one day

The week start day is a Workplace setting (set once in Settings; default Monday). The manager schedule grid, week navigation, worker week lists, day rosters, and timecard week totals all derive their weeks from that single stored value — no surface may silently fall back to a different week start. A Shift — including one that crosses midnight, like 6:00 PM–1:00 AM — belongs to exactly one day: the day it starts, and every surface renders it on that day with an explicit "+1" marker rather than repeating it on the next day. When HotSchedules lost both of these behaviors after its acquisition, workers saw shifts on the wrong days and managers could not keep a Sunday week pinned; duplicating overnight shifts onto two day columns double-counts hours in a worker's head and erodes trust in the schedule as the single source of truth.
