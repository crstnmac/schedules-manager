# Target hourly teams in every industry

jooling is a fast HotSchedules-style scheduler for hourly work, not a restaurant-only product. Restaurant teams remain a valid customer, but Workplace, Location, Position, and Schedule are industry-agnostic so retail, healthcare, logistics, and other shift-based workplaces use the same operating model. Position Section (FOH/BOH) is removed; Managers filter staff with Worker Groups instead. This supersedes ADR 0004; ADR 0002's boundary of owning the schedule rather than layering over another scheduler still holds, and ADR 0003's geographic concentration is unchanged.
