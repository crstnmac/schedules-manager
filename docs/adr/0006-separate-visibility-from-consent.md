# Separate acknowledgement from shift acceptance

Acknowledgement records only that a Worker saw a Published Schedule or Material Schedule Change; it never establishes consent, availability, or acceptance of responsibility. The Worker must explicitly acknowledge rather than being marked as acknowledged when a screen loads, and Shift Acceptance remains a separate explicit action. Combining visibility with agreement—or inferring either passively—would create misleading records, confuse workers, and weaken trust when schedules change.
