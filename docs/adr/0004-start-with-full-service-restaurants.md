# Start with full-service restaurants

The initial segment will be independent full-service restaurants and small full-service restaurant groups rather than every food-service model. Full-service restaurants were the largest food-service category in Austin's 2022 Small Business Needs Assessment, and their varied positions, service periods, daily rosters, and frequent shift exchanges provide a strong test of the product's core scheduling promise; limited-service and other models can follow after retention is demonstrated.
