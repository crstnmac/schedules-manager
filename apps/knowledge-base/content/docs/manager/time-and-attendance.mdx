## Your clock

**Clock** is your own time clock. If you are scheduled yourself, clock in for your assigned published Shift there; the page also lists the week's published Shifts and your recent punches.

![Clock page with your punches](/screenshots/clock.png)

Workers normally clock in and out from their own app. Settings may permit an early start, require the Worker to be within the Location geofence, or round punch times.

## Kiosk

The shared **Kiosk** at `/kiosk` needs two PINs: the **Location PIN** and the Worker's own PIN. No personal sign-in is used. Protect the device and rotate a PIN if it is exposed.

![Location kiosk with location and worker PIN fields](/screenshots/kiosk.png)

## Daily Roster

**Roster** shows published Shifts for the selected date across your Locations, with search and print support. It is a read-only view — to edit the day, work in Schedule.

![Daily roster with print support](/screenshots/roster.png)

Attendance Marks such as **Late**, **No-show**, or **Sick** are recorded in the shift editor on the Schedule page under **Today operations**. They do not change the Published Schedule or Time Entries.

## Timesheets

**Timesheets** lists completed Time Entries with their clock window, hours, and status. Approve or decline entries — individually or in bulk. Entries can show an **Auto closed** badge when the auto clock-out rule ended them.

![Timesheets approval list](/screenshots/timesheets.png)

Punch times can be corrected in the shift editor on the Schedule page, and corrections require a reason. Labor Cost from Wage Rates and overtime, plus manual Daily Sales, live in **Reports**. jooling does not run payroll.
