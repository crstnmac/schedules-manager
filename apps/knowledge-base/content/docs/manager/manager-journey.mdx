## Weekly planning

1. Review Time-off Requests, Unavailability, Work Preferences, and holidays before you plan.
2. Open **Schedule** and choose a Location and workweek.
3. Add Shifts with **Add**, or reuse what already works — **Copy last week**, a saved Schedule Template, a Shift Pattern, or **Auto-assign open shifts**.
4. Check the grid: each day shows scheduled hours and cost, and each worker shows their weekly hours. Conflict chips mark overlaps, approved time off, Unavailability, and access problems.
5. Select **Publish**, review the confirmation, and publish the complete draft.
6. Monitor delivery, Acknowledgements, and required Shift Acceptances from Overview and Coverage.

## Daily operations

- **Overview** shows the current staffing picture, open shifts, conflicts, and what needs attention.
- **Roster** shows today's published shifts across your Locations and supports printing.
- **Clock** is your own time clock, and **Timesheets** holds completed Time Entries to approve or decline.
- **Coverage** holds releases, pickups, and swaps that need a decision.
- **Time off** holds requests and leave administration.
- **Announcements**, **Messages**, **Activity**, and **Reports** support communication and review.

Attendance Marks such as **Late**, **No-show**, and **Sick** are added in the shift editor on the Schedule page, under **Today operations**.

Acknowledgement never means consent to changed work. A release or swap never moves responsibility before final approval and publication.
