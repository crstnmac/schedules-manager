- **Announcements** are Workplace-wide notices. Posting one notifies everyone at the Workplace.
- **Messages** are conversations between people in the Workplace: an **Everyone** thread plus private direct threads.
- **Inbox** (inside **Activity**) is the durable record of notifications; push is a best-effort alert.
- Invitation email exposes its delivery state to Managers in the Workers directory.

![Announcements list with posting](/screenshots/announcements.png)

![Messages with the Everyone thread and direct threads](/screenshots/messages.png)

“Sent” means a message left jooling, not that a person saw it. “Delivered” means the email or push service accepted it. Only an explicit Acknowledgement records that a Worker selected **I saw this**.

## Activity

**Activity** has two parts: **Inbox**, your notification list with read states, and **Audit trail**, a filterable record of manager actions such as publications, reminders, coverage decisions, attendance marks, and time-entry edits. The audit trail can be downloaded as CSV.

![Activity inbox with unread notifications](/screenshots/activity.png)

![Audit trail with filters and CSV export](/screenshots/activity-audit.png)

Managers can send a reminder to Workers who have not acknowledged a published Schedule. If the result is uncertain after a connection problem, check Activity before sending another reminder.

## Reports

**Reports** covers scheduled and worked hours, Labor Cost, manual Daily Sales, labor percentage, coverage, and requests — organized into **Hours & labor**, **Coverage**, **Requests**, and **Leave** tabs. Worked hours and attendance columns export as CSV.

![Hours and labor report with charts](/screenshots/reports.png)

![Coverage report with fill rate and utilization](/screenshots/reports-coverage.png)

The **Requests** tab summarizes time-off, release, pickup, and swap decisions with approval rates; the **Leave** tab tracks balances and can export a leave payroll CSV.

![Requests report with approval rates](/screenshots/reports-requests.png)

![Leave report with balances](/screenshots/reports-leave.png)

These are operational reports, not payroll records.
