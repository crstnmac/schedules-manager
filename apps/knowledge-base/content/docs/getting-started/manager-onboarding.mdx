title: Manager onboarding
description: Create the first workplace and prepare it for scheduling.
---

## 1. Create an account and choose the manager path

Sign up on web with the **Create account** tab. You are then asked how you are joining — choose **I manage a workplace**. People waiting for an invitation should take the worker path instead.

![Create account screen](/screenshots/sign-up.png)

## 2. Set up the Workplace

Enter the Workplace name plus its first Location — name, address, time zone — and a first Position. The creator becomes the first Manager. You can add more Locations, Positions, and people later in Settings.

![Workplace setup form](/screenshots/onboarding-manager.png)

<Callout type="warn" title="Use a named time zone">
Do not use a fixed UTC offset. Overnight work and daylight-saving transitions depend on the Location's IANA time zone.
</Callout>

## 3. Review the rules

Open **Settings** to check scheduling and approval rules, the Notice Window for late changes, time-clock behavior, leave types and policies, holidays, templates, notifications, and your subscription.

## 4. Invite the team

Invite Workers by email or CSV from **Workers** and assign Location and Position access. A Worker becomes active after accepting the invitation. Pending invitations can be resent, and their email delivery state appears in the directory.

![Worker directory with invitations](/screenshots/workers.png)

## 5. Publish the first week

Continue to [Build and publish schedules](/manager/scheduling). Before publication, Workers see an intentional empty state rather than draft work.
