## Mobile tabs

- **Schedule** leads with your next Shift and required responses.
- **Calendar** shows more of your current, upcoming, and historical schedule.
- **Open shifts** lists work available for pickup.
- **Inbox** holds scheduling and request notifications.
- **More** opens availability, time off, Timecard, messages, announcements, team, and account actions available to you.

Manager Employments see a different mobile tab set: Overview, Schedule, Requests when permitted, Inbox, and More.

## Worker web

The worker web side navigation groups the same areas: **My schedule**, **Timecard**, and **Open shifts** under My work; **Time off & availability** under Requests; and **Messages**, **Announcements**, and **Inbox** under Team. Timecard appears only when time operations are enabled for the Workplace.

## Shift details

On mobile, open a Shift to see Location, Position, time, status, tasks, Time Entry state, release options, and any change requiring response. The worker web shows the same information in lists and sections — the week's table, **Pending changes**, **Shift tasks**, and **Requests** — rather than a single shift page.
