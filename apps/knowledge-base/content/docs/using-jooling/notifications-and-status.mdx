## Where notifications appear

Inbox is the durable list inside jooling. Mobile push alerts draw attention to new items, and invitation email helps a person join. Push and email may be delayed or disabled, so always use the current schedule and Inbox when confirming work.

![Worker Inbox with notifications](/screenshots/worker-inbox.png)

## Delivery words

- **Sent**: jooling handed the message to the delivery service.
- **Delivered**: that service accepted or reported delivery.
- **Acknowledged**: the Worker explicitly selected **I saw this**.

Delivery does not equal Acknowledgement. Opening a schedule does not automatically acknowledge it.

## Required actions

Unread notifications and required schedule responses are different. Marking an Inbox item read clears its unread state, but it does not acknowledge a Schedule, accept a Shift, approve a request, or finish another action. Follow the notification to the relevant screen and use the named action there.

Managers can remind Workers who have not acknowledged a publication. Managers can also follow each publication's delivery state — who was sent the schedule, who received it, and who has seen it — from the Schedule page. Before sending a second reminder after a connection problem, check Activity and the delivery status.
