The side navigation is grouped by job. **Activity** and **Settings** sit at the bottom, below the groups.

| Group | Area | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Overview | This week's staffing picture, conflicts, open shifts, and what needs attention |
| Plan | Schedule | Create drafts, assign Shifts, review conflicts, and publish |
| Plan | Roster | Today's published Shifts across your Locations, with print |
| Plan | Coverage | Decide releases, pickups, and swaps |
| Team | Workers | Invitations, Employment details, wages, PINs, and documents |
| Team | Time off | Review requests and leave decisions |
| Team | Messages | Workplace conversations |
| Team | Announcements | Workplace-wide notices |
| Time | Clock | Your own Time Entries and punches |
| Time | Timesheets | Approve or decline completed Time Entries |
| Time | Reports | Attendance, hours and labor, coverage, requests, leave, and CSV exports |
| — | Activity | Your notification Inbox and the audit trail |
| — | Settings | Configure Workplace rules and reusable scheduling tools |

![Overview with the manager side navigation](/screenshots/overview.png)

The side navigation only shows areas allowed by your role and capabilities. Some operational areas appear only when the Workplace enables the corresponding feature. On narrow screens, use the menu button to open navigation.

Use the profile menu to switch theme or sign out. If an expected area is missing, confirm the current Workplace and ask a Manager with access to review your permissions.
