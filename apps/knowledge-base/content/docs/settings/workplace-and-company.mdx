---
title: Workplace and company
description: Configure the Workplace name, week, pay, labor, notice, and team visibility.
---

Open **Settings → General** for the core Workplace settings used by schedules and reports:

- **Identity** — the Workplace name.
- **Week & pay** — the start of week and the pay period (weekly, every two weeks, semimonthly, or monthly) with its anchor date.
- **Labor** — weekly and daily overtime thresholds, the labor cost goal, and whether Managers can view labor cost.
- **Late changes** — the Notice Window that decides when a changed Shift needs a Worker's explicit acceptance.

![Workplace general settings with week, pay, labor, and notice window](/screenshots/settings-general.png)

Changing the week start affects how weekly Schedules are grouped; make the change between planning cycles when possible. Workplace settings never rewrite previously published Schedule Versions.

Open **Settings → Company** for Workplace-wide behavior: whether Messaging, Announcements, and Shift tasks are available, and whether worker contact details are visible.

![Company settings with feature toggles](/screenshots/settings-company.png)

## Before saving

1. Tell Managers when a week or labor setting will change.
2. Finish or review active drafts that depend on the current week structure.
3. Save the setting.
4. Open a future week or the affected communication area to confirm the result.
