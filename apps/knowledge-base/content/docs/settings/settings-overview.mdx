Settings is available to Managers with at least one relevant management capability. You may see only the sections your role permits.

![Settings with the section navigation](/screenshots/settings-general.png)

## Categories

- **Workplace** covers subscription, general details, company behavior, Locations, Positions, and connected services.
- **Policies** covers Schedule (visibility, exchanges, and automatic approvals), Time clock, Time off, and Leave policies.
- **Team** contains Groups, Schedule Teams, and Leave Types.
- **Schedule catalog** contains Shift Templates, Shift Patterns, Tags, Time Blocks, and Holidays.
- **You** contains your display and notification preferences.

Changes can affect future scheduling, Worker eligibility, request routing, or time calculations. Read the explanation beside a setting, save intentionally, and verify the result in the relevant workflow. Do not change policy merely to force one exceptional request through; use the workflow’s override or decision reason when provided.
