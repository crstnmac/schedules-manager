---
title: Schedule and approval policies
description: Control visibility, shift exchanges, rest rules, and manager decisions.
---

Use **Settings → Schedule** for what Workers see and how Shifts move between people:

- **Visibility** — whether workers see everyone's shifts or only their own, whether others' approved time off is visible, and how far ahead planned shifts appear.
- **Shift exchanges** — whether releases, pickups, and swaps are available at all.
- **Automatic approvals** — pickups, swaps, releases, and late schedule changes can take effect immediately or wait for a Manager decision.
- **Breaks & compliance** — minimum rest between shifts and the maximum run of consecutive workdays.
- **Restrictions** — whether Unavailability needs approval before it counts.

![Schedule policy settings](/screenshots/settings-schedule-policies.png)

The Notice Window — when a new or materially changed Shift needs a Worker's explicit acceptance — lives under **Settings → General → Late changes**.

![Notice window under General settings](/screenshots/settings-general-notice-window.png)

## Approval policies

Use **Settings → Approval policies** to create Approval Policy Groups. Each group chooses, per request type — Time off, Unavailability, Shift release, Shift pickup, Shift swap — whether a Manager decision is required. A group is attached to a Schedule week on the Schedule page; weeks without a group fall back to the workplace default.

![Approval policies with request type toggles](/screenshots/settings-approval-policies.png)

## Policy principles

- Acknowledgement and Shift Acceptance always remain separate.
- A release or swap does not transfer responsibility before final approval.
- Shortening the Notice Window affects future change classification; it does not erase existing responses.
- Approval settings should match how the Workplace actually operates.

After changing a policy, walk through one representative request or future draft with another Manager before relying on it broadly.
