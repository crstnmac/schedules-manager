---
title: Time clock settings
description: Configure early starts, rounding, grace, geofence, and auto clock-out.
---

Time clock settings determine when and where Workers can start Time Entries and how punches are read:

- **Early clock-in** — how many minutes before a published Shift a Worker may clock in.
- **Clock rounding** — round punch times to the nearest number of minutes, or keep them exact.
- **Late arrival grace** — minutes after the Shift start that still do not count as late.
- **Auto clock-out grace** — minutes after the Shift end before an open entry is closed automatically.
- **Require geofence** — clock-ins must report coordinates within the Location radius (set per Location). Coordinates are self-reported by the device, so treat this as an advisory check and review punches on the timesheet.
- **Timesheet notes** — allow notes on Time Entries.

![Time clock settings with rounding and grace rules](/screenshots/settings-time-clock.png)

The shared **Kiosk** is not configured here: its Location PIN belongs to each Location, the Worker PIN to each Worker's Employment page, and the clock itself lives at `/kiosk`.

Before enabling geofence, verify each Location's coordinates and radius and tell Workers why location permission is needed. Always provide a Manager path for legitimate device or location failures.

Policy changes should be tested on a non-critical Shift. Managers can correct a punch with a reason from the Schedule's shift editor, and corrections appear in operational history.
