## Personal preferences

Under **Settings → Preferences**, control how names and times appear for you. They do not change another person's account or the Location time zone used to interpret Shifts.

![Personal preferences](/screenshots/settings-preferences.png)

## Notifications

Under **Settings → Notifications**, choose which supported events should alert you. Device-level notification permission and jooling notification preferences both affect push alerts. Turning off push does not remove Inbox records or required actions.

![Notification preferences](/screenshots/settings-notifications.png)

## Subscription

Managers with billing access can open **Settings → Subscription** to see the current plan, billing state, renewal date, and paid-location usage. Plan cards show the Schedule and Operations tiers with monthly or annual pricing per Location. **Manage billing** opens the Polar customer portal for payment details and invoices; checkout starts a Polar subscription flow.

![Subscription page with plan and billing state](/screenshots/settings-subscription.png)

Billing belongs to the Workplace; Workers do not pay for their Employment access. If the Workplace is locked behind a billing requirement, a Manager with billing access must resolve it — do not create a second Workplace to work around a subscription issue.
