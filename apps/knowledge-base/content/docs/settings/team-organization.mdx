---
title: Team organization
description: Use Groups, Schedule Teams, Tags, and Employment scope.
---

**Groups** are named sets of Workers used to filter the Schedule — for example Front of House and Back of House. They never change anyone's permissions.

![Worker groups](/screenshots/settings-groups.png)

**Schedule Teams** support parallel scheduling organization within a Location, each with its own color. Use them when one Location operates multiple scheduling lanes that Managers need to view separately.

![Schedule teams with colors](/screenshots/settings-schedule-teams.png)

**Tags** label Shifts for filtering or operational context. A Tag does not replace a Position and does not grant Worker eligibility.

![Shift tags](/screenshots/settings-tags.png)

Employment **Location and Position access** determines where and in which role a Worker can be scheduled or pick up work. That scope is set on the invitation and shown on each Worker's Employment page — not by placing them in a Group.

Choose names that Workers and Managers already use. Avoid creating multiple Groups or Tags with overlapping meanings; this makes schedule filters difficult to trust.
