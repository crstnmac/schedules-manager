---
title: Coverage and swaps
description: Release, pick up, or exchange shifts without losing accountability.
---

To give up assigned work, find the shift in **My schedule** and select **Request release**. Responsibility does not change while the request is pending — you keep the shift until a Manager approves a replacement and a new version is published.

![My schedule with propose swap and request release actions](/screenshots/worker-home.png)

To take open work, open **Open shifts**, review the Location, Position, date, and time, and select **Request pickup**. jooling checks Employment state, scope, overlaps, Unavailability, and eligibility; a Manager makes the assignment. Requests show as **Waiting on manager**, **Assigned to you**, or **Declined**.

![Open shifts available for pickup](/screenshots/worker-openshifts.png)

A swap requires a proposal, counterpart agreement, and Manager approval. Select **Propose swap** on one of your shifts, choose a coworker, and send the proposal. Both people keep their original work until it is approved and published.

![Propose a shift swap dialog](/screenshots/worker-swap-dialog.png)

After approval, verify the new Published Schedule Version in **My schedule** or your **Inbox** rather than relying only on a push alert.
