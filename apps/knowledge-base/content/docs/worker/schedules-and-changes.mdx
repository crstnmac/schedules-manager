Workers see immutable Published Schedule Versions. Manager drafts remain private, and history stays available after a later version replaces a week — open it from **Earlier weeks**.

![A published week in history](/screenshots/worker-history.png)

## Acknowledgement

Select **I saw this** only to record visibility. Opening the schedule does not acknowledge it. Acknowledgement says nothing about consent or availability.

## Shift Acceptance

A new or materially changed Shift inside the Workplace Notice Window may require explicit acceptance. The **Pending changes** section lists what changed, with **Accept shift** and **Decline** for each item. This remains separate from Acknowledgement so visibility and agreement cannot be confused.

Delivery moves through Sent, Delivered, and Acknowledged for Managers. Push or email delivery may lag; the published schedule inside jooling is authoritative — check **My schedule** rather than relying on an alert.
