---
title: Time clock and communication
description: Track worked time and stay informed.
---

On **My schedule**, the top bar shows your next Shift with its **Clock in** and **Clock out** controls. Clock-in typically opens shortly before the Shift starts. During work, open the clock menu to record a Break and to finish at the end of work.

![Timecard with entries and weekly total](/screenshots/worker-timecard.png)

If the network fails, reconnect and verify the visible clock state before tapping again. If you are unsure whether the action was saved, ask a Manager to check their Clock or Timesheets view. When your Workplace requires a geofence, allow location permission and be inside the Location boundary when you start; on a desktop browser without location access, ask a Manager or use the Kiosk.

**Timecard** lists every Time Entry you started and finished, with durations and status. Breaks you recorded appear with the entry.

Inbox is the durable notification list; push is a convenience. Announcements are Workplace-wide notices and Messages are Workplace conversations. Neither replaces the Published Schedule as the source of truth.

Shift Tasks for your next shift appear on My schedule. Completing a task neither finishes a Time Entry nor acknowledges a schedule change.
