---
title: Availability and time off
description: Tell managers when you cannot work and what you prefer.
---

Open **Time off & availability**. The page has four tabs: **Time off**, **Forecast**, **When I can't work**, and **Preferences**.

Use **When I can't work** for a **weekly window** (for example, every Sunday) or a **date exception** for a specific day. These are hard constraints — a Manager must record an override reason to schedule through one.

Use **Preferences** to describe desired times or Locations in your own words. They guide planning but never guarantee or block assignment.

Use **Time off** for a specific interval. Choose a Leave Type and, when your policy requires it, attach a document such as a doctor's note (PDF or image). Pending, approved, and declined requests stay visible, and your remaining balances show in hours next to each type.

![Availability page with balances, a pending request, and unavailability](/screenshots/worker-availability.png)

The **Forecast** tab projects how your balances develop over the coming months.

<Callout type="info">
Time off is not the same as releasing an already published Shift. Use **Request release** on My schedule for an existing assignment.
</Callout>
