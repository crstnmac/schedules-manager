---
title: Worker journey
description: The complete day-to-day flow for a worker.
---

Accept the Workplace invitation, sign in, and configure notification permission. Add Unavailability, Work Preferences, and Time-off Requests before planning begins.

**My schedule** leads with your next Shift in the top bar and your current published week. The side list takes you to next week, earlier weeks, pending changes, shift tasks, requests, and swaps. Draft changes never appear.

![My schedule with the current week and rail sections](/screenshots/worker-home.png)

Use **I saw this** to acknowledge a publication. If a late material change requires agreement, the **Pending changes** section shows **Accept shift** or **Decline** separately. Acknowledging never accepts responsibility.

When plans change, use **Request release** on one of your shifts, browse **Open shifts**, request pickup, or propose a swap. You remain responsible for assigned work until a Manager approves the change and publishes a new version.

During work, the top bar shows **Clock in** and **Clock out** for your current shift, and **Timecard** records your finished Time Entries and Breaks. Review Shift Tasks, Announcements, Inbox, and Messages as needed.

![Timecard with completed entries](/screenshots/worker-timecard.png)
