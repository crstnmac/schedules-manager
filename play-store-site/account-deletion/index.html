Account deletion

Request deletion of your jooling account

You can request deletion of your jooling account and associated account data at any time. Email our privacy team from the email address on your account to verify the request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@jooling.com from the email address associated with your jooling account.
  2. Use the subject line jooling account deletion request.
  3. Tell us you want your account deleted. Do not include your password.

Data deleted and data retained

After we verify and process the request, we close the account and delete or anonymize its account and profile data within 90 days. This includes account identifiers and profile details such as your name and contact information.

Workplace records, including schedules, shifts, time entries, and workplace messages, are controlled by your employer. Contact your employer to request deletion of those records. They are kept while the workplace is active and deleted or anonymized within 90 days after workplace closure so the workplace can export its records first.

We retain billing and tax records for up to 7 years where required by law, consent records for at least 3 years, and application and device logs for up to 90 days. We keep other information only as needed for legal obligations or an open dispute.

Response time

We respond within 45 days of a verifiable request. Complex requests may take up to an additional 45 days; we will explain any extension.
